Cookies and local storage

Effective: 23 October 2026 Operator: Avenir, Kenya. Contact: avenirtech001@gmail.com

Receiver website and policy pages

These pages do not set advertising cookies or run analytics/tracking pixels. System fonts and locally hosted artwork avoid font/image-CDN requests. The download button requests the receiver ZIP from Supabase Storage only when selected. GitHub Pages and the download host receive ordinary request information such as IP address and user agent to deliver content and protect their services; see https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement and the VSW privacy policy.

The download page can store vsw-download-motion in localStorage only when you choose its motion setting. It stores on/off, not a user identity, and remembers the visual setting you requested until you change it or clear site data. If browser storage is blocked the page remains usable without saving the preference. The policy pages have no scripts and do not create this preference.

Private Control Room

The owner's private dashboard stores vsw-admin-session in sessionStorage for the requested signed-in session. Sign out clears it. Access is checked by the backend and the session expires. This is not an advertising or marketing cookie. Any private hosting sign-in protections may use their own essential authentication storage, controlled by that hosting provider.

Choices

Use your browser's site-data controls to clear stored settings or sign out of the private dashboard. There is no nonessential advertising/analytics cookie category to accept or reject on these public pages, so a blanket accept-all cookie banner would be misleading. If nonessential tracking is introduced later it must stay off until a separate lawful consent process is implemented. Android SDK advertising privacy choices are handled in the app; this website notice does not replace them.
