Account and data deletion

Request help without installing or signing in: Email Avenir about account deletion. Never include a password or sign-in code.

Effective: 23 October 2026 Operator: Avenir, Kenya Contact: avenirtech001@gmail.com

Registered account: In VSW open Account, Manage account, Delete account. Sign in as the account owner, read the confirmation and confirm deletion. The app must receive an explicit server deletion result before claiming success.

Without the app or if you cannot sign in: email avenirtech001@gmail.com from your registered account email with subject "VSW account deletion request". State whether you want the whole account deleted or help with particular data. Do not send a password, sign-in code, identity document or payment-card details. Avenir will verify ownership before acting on private information; an email request is not an instant automatic deletion.

Cloud deletion removes the account, profile, private pad backups, profile-image files, presence record and account-linked premium/purchase records stored by VSW. Necessary provider security logs, protected backups and records that must be retained under applicable law may remain under their retention arrangements; contact Avenir for details. Deleting the cloud account does not erase pads or settings on another device or cancel a store transaction. Account deletion does not automatically request a refund.

Registered guest: On the device holding that guest session, use Account, Delete guest cloud record. A local guest who never registered has no VSW guest cloud account to delete. If the guest session was lost, contact support; anonymous ownership may not be verifiable from an email alone. Never clear the saved guest session before requesting its deletion if you need that session to prove ownership.

Local data only: Settings, Delete local data erases this installation's pads, settings, saved sign-in data, credits and privacy choices, then closes VSW. It does not delete the cloud account. Delete the cloud account first if you want both. Keep a backup of pads you wish to retain.
