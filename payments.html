Payments and refunds

Effective: 24 October 2026 Operator: Avenir, Kenya Support: avenirtech001@gmail.com

Current release

Google Play checkout is not configured. You cannot buy Premium from this build and Avenir does not collect a card number or bank details in VSW. The Windows receiver download is free. Do not pay through a personal account, email or unofficial link to activate Premium.

When store billing becomes available

The store must show the actual localized price, currency, applicable taxes and whether the offer is a one-time purchase before you confirm. Lifetime Premium is intended as a one-time entitlement to unlimited play time and eligible published pad downloads, tied to your registered VSW account after server verification. It is not a recurring subscription. Guest users must sign in or create an eligible account before checkout. This description does not mean checkout is available now.

Refund help

For an actual future Google Play purchase, use the store's refund process and your order history. See https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637 . Store rules and applicable consumer law determine eligibility; Avenir does not impose an absolute no-refunds rule. Contact support for duplicate charges, missing entitlements or assistance. Provide the order reference only when needed through a private support conversation; never send payment-card details or passwords. Account deletion does not automatically refund or cancel a store purchase. Successful refunds/revocations may remove the corresponding premium entitlement.

Play time and ads

Rewarded time is an in-app usage credit, not money, a deposit or a cash balance. It cannot be cashed out. Watch ad is optional: closing before the SDK grants its reward adds no time. The terms explain the balance thresholds and rolling cap before ads are used. Ad availability is not guaranteed. There is no hidden fee for the receiver download or for reading policy/deletion information.
